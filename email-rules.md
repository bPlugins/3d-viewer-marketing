# Halloween Email — Copy Rules

## Audience
- The email goes to **free users**.
- We don't know what they sell, so don't target a specific niche. Keep examples general enough to fit any store or business.

## Structure
- Follow **Problem → Solution → Benefit**.
- The email can open with a question.
- max 2 links, one CTA and one link.
- Describe the solution first **without naming the plugin**. Introduce our plugin only after that, as the way to get that solution.

## Tone & Language
- Use business language.
- Use business scenarios to show the problem and the benefit.

## Discount
- Don't put the discount percentage in the email body. It can go in the subject line.
- End the email with a line like: *"Want a bigger discount? Contact us."* but not for all emails. 

---

## Added by Claude (suggestions — keep, edit or remove)
- **One clear CTA.** Use a single main call to action (e.g. "Upgrade to Pro"). The "contact us" line at the end is the only other link.
- **Keep it short.** Aim for about 150–200 words so it's easy to read on a phone.
- **Light Halloween theme.** Keep Halloween to the subject line and the opening line, and let the business message carry the rest. No gimmicky puns in the body.
- **Show urgency with a deadline.** State when the offer ends (date) rather than using pressure words like "hurry" or "last chance".
- **Benefits over features.** Name a feature only when it's tied to an outcome (more sales, fewer returns, more trust from buyers).
- **Subject line under ~50 characters** and a preview text line that adds to the subject instead of repeating it.
- **Brand check.** Use only this brand's name, links and signature. Don't mix in another brand's plugins or attribution.

Email 4 — Day 7
Subject line

Show your whole range in one viewer

Preview text

Several 3D models, one thumbnail strip, one clean page.

Body

How do you show several products, or several versions of one product, without cluttering the page?

One viewer per model means a long, slow page and visitors who stop scrolling halfway. The model they would have liked most is often the one they never reach.

Imagine one viewer that holds them all. Visitors switch between models with a thumbnail strip or arrows, and each model keeps its own camera angle, lighting, hotspots and AR.

3D Viewer Pro does this with multi-model galleries. Each color, size or product can be its own model in the same viewer.

One tidy page that presents everything. When your range grows, you add a model to the gallery instead of building a new section.

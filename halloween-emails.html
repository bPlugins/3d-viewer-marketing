Will it fit? Show them instead of telling them

Preview text

Body

How many times a week does someone ask you how big a product really is?

No tricks this Halloween, just a straight answer to that question.

A product page lists dimensions in a specification table. Shoppers read the numbers and still can't picture them. So they email you, or they guess, or they order and find out at the door that it doesn't fit. Each of those costs you time, and the last one costs you a return.

Picture a different page. The visitor taps a button and a ruler appears on the 3D model, showing width, height and depth in millimeters, centimeters, meters, inches or feet, whichever you choose. Then they tap View in AR, point their phone at the room, and the product stands there at its real size. Desktop visitors scan a QR code and carry on from their phone.

3D Viewer Pro adds both to your WooCommerce product pages: a real-world dimensions ruler that can read the product's own shipping dimensions, and AR that works on iPhone and Android with no app.

The benefit is fewer "how big is it" emails, fewer surprises on delivery and buyers who order with confidence.
